Parallax TV is a free, minimal streaming hub built for PC. No clutter, minimal ads, just a smooth way to find something good and press play.

## Features

- **Search anything.** Find any movie or show by name, or jump straight in with a TMDB ID or link. Your recent searches are one click away.
- **Movies.** A featured spotlight, what's trending, what's in theaters, a premiere calendar of what's coming soon, and the Top 10 highest rated films of all time.
- **TV Shows.** Channel-style spotlight, trending shows, fresh episodes airing today and this week, upcoming premieres, and the best rated series ever made.
- **Continue Watching.** Pick up exactly where you left off, with time left and progress on every episode.
- **Navigator.** A quick hub to move between Search, Movies, TV Shows and Settings, with live previews of each.
- **Player settings.** Autoplay, seek time, quality and source preferences, with a live preview of every change.
- **Website quality.** Low, Medium or High effects, or Auto, which watches how smoothly the site runs on your device and adjusts for you.
- **Friendly errors.** Clear notices when you go offline, when movie info can't load or when something breaks, with a one-click way to try again.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `1` | Search |
| `2` | Movies |
| `3` | TV Shows |
| `4` | Settings |
| `Esc` | Close the navigator |

## Requirements

Parallax TV is designed for desktop and laptop browsers. A recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari is recommended. Mobile support is coming soon.

## Status

Parallax TV is in **Pre-Alpha**. Features may change and you may run into bugs. Feedback and suggestions are always welcome.
